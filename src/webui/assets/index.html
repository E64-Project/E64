Datasette Help

LOAD opens File Manager. Select a TAP image and attach it as tape.

To load from tape, enter LOAD on the C64 and press RETURN, then press PLAY. PLAY remains pressed while the C64 controls the tape motor. The green LED indicates the motor signal, not tape movement.

REW rewinds the tape; F.FWD winds it forward. STOP stops the transport and releases the pressed keys.

RECORD engages REC and PLAY together and requires a writable TAP image. Use the C64 SAVE command to record data.

EJECT detaches the tape image and resets the displayed counter to 000.

Counter RESET sets the counter to 000 without ejecting or rewinding the tape.

Reset Tape With Machine Reset controls whether a machine reset also resets the tape position and counter. Counter RESET does not change this preference.
